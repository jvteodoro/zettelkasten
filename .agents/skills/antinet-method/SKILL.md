# antinet-method

1. Read docs/00-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md and docs/12-BOOK-TRACEABILITY.md.
2. Distinguish book requirements from engineered interpretation in PR.
3. Reject link-following, backlinks, autocomplete, full-text search, tags, suggestions and auto-filing in daily UI.
4. Test selective Index and intentional adjacent-card browsing.
5. Do not claim typing reproduces handwriting effects. Record uncertain choices in an ADR.
